Contour Memory Foam Pillow

Contoured memory foam pillow, 60 × 40 cm, medium-firm, with a zip-off bamboo knit cover.

The Contour Memory Foam Pillow is shaped for people who sleep on their side or back and wake with a stiff neck. A gentle curve along the front edge keeps the head in line with the spine, while the slow-recovery foam eases into the shape of your neck over the first few minutes and holds it until you turn. There is no lump to punch into place and nothing to plump in the morning.

The core is a single block of open-cell memory foam, poured rather than cut from offcuts, so the density is the same from edge to edge. Open cells let warm air move out through the sides instead of building up under your head, which matters more in a Mumbai May than any cooling gel coating. The cover is a knitted bamboo viscose blend that zips off for washing.

We sell one size, the standard 60 by 40 cm, in a medium-firm feel. It suits most side and back sleepers between 50 and 90 kg. Stomach sleepers usually find any contour pillow too tall and are better off with our low fibre pillow, which is listed in the comparison further down this page.

Every pillow leaves the factory vacuum packed and rolled. It arrives in a box about the size of a shoebox, expands to full height within an hour of unrolling and reaches its final feel after a night or two. A faint new-foam smell is normal for the first day and clears with the window open.

Why we picked it

  • Even density: one poured block, so the left and right sides feel the same and there is no soft spot in the middle after a month.
  • Real airflow: open-cell foam and a knitted cover let heat out through the sides; no gel layer that stops working after a season.
  • Washable cover: the outer cover zips off and goes in the machine; the inner liner keeps sweat off the foam.
  • A shape that stays: the contour is cast into the foam, not stitched into the cover, so it does not flatten as the cover ages.

The first nights

  1. Unroll the pillow on a flat surface and take off the plastic. It will start to expand at once.
  2. Leave it for an hour before use, ideally near an open window so the new-foam smell can clear.
  3. Put the high edge under your neck and the low edge towards the top of your head if you sleep on your back; side sleepers usually prefer the high edge only.
  4. Give it two or three nights. Memory foam feels firmer than a fibre pillow at first and your neck needs a little time to settle into the new height.
  5. If the height is too much, take out the thin base sheet inside the cover to lower it by about 1 cm.

Care

  • Wash the zip-off cover at 30 °C with a mild detergent and let it air dry. Tumble drying shrinks the knit.
  • Never wash or soak the foam core. Water breaks the cell walls and the pillow will not recover its shape.
  • For a mark on the foam, dab it with a cloth wrung out in soapy water, then leave the pillow in the shade to dry fully before putting the cover back.
  • Air the pillow for an hour every couple of weeks. Direct sun for long periods yellows the foam and makes it brittle.
  • Use a pillowcase over the cover. It keeps skin oils off the knit and cuts washing to once a month.
  • Store it flat in a dry cupboard if you are away for a while. Do not roll it again once it has expanded.
  • Keep it away from heaters and hot water bottles. Foam softens above 60 °C and can lose its shape for good.

Questions and answers

Is it too tall for a stomach sleeper?
Usually, yes. Stomach sleepers do best with a pillow under 8 cm; this one is 9 cm at its lowest edge. The fibre pillow in the comparison below is a better fit.
Does it get hot?
Less than a solid-block memory foam pillow. Open cells and the knitted cover move air out through the sides, though no foam pillow is as cool as a feather one under a fan.
Can I wash the whole pillow?
Only the cover. The foam core must stay dry; a damp cloth is fine for a small mark.
Which way round does it go?
High edge under the neck for back sleepers, with the low edge towards the crown of the head. Side sleepers can use either edge, most prefer the high one.
Does it fit a normal pillowcase?
Yes. It is the standard 60 by 40 cm size and slips into any regular case, though a snug knit case shows the contour best.
How long does the smell last?
A faint smell for the first day, gone by the second with the window open. It comes from the vacuum packing, not the foam itself.
What if I do not like it?
Sleep on it for up to 30 nights. If it is not for you, write to us and we collect it from your address and refund the full price, cover included.
How long will it last?
Three to four years of nightly use before the foam starts to soften noticeably. The warranty covers sagging of more than 2 cm in the first 24 months.
Is the foam safe?
The foam is CertiPUR certified, made without heavy metals or formaldehyde, and the cover is OEKO-TEX Standard 100.
Can I lower the height?
Yes. A thin base sheet inside the cover can be removed to drop the height by about 1 cm at both edges.
